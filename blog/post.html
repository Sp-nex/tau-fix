Solar Panel installation Cost in Kerala

Complete Guide to solar pricing, saving and installation in Kerala

Solar energy is becoming increasingly popular in kerala due to rising electricity costs and growing awareness about renewable energy. Installing solar panels can significantly reduce monthly electricity bills while increasing long-term energy independence.

Average Solar Panel Cost in Kerala

The average residential solar installation Cost in Kerala usually ranges from 1 lakh to 5 lakhs depending on system size, panel quality, inverter type and installation complexity

Small homes generally require 1kW to 3kW systems while commercial building may need larger capacities.

Factors Affecting Solar installation Price

  • Solar panel Brand and efficiency
  • Roof size and structure
  • Battery backup requirements
  • Inverter quality
  • installation complexity
  • Government subsidy availability

Government Subsidy for Solar in Kerala

Kerala residents may qualify for solar subsidies under central and state renewable energy programs. Subsidy availability changes periodically based on Government policies.
